Privacy Policy — “Its Neiron” app

Last updated: 1 November 2026

“Its Neiron” is a private tool used only by its owner to publish the owner’s own short videos (Reels) to the owner’s own Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts through the Meta Graph API. It is not offered to the public and no other people log in to it.

What data the app uses

Only the access tokens and IDs of the owner’s own Facebook Pages and linked Instagram accounts, needed to upload and publish videos, and the IDs and links of the posts it creates. The app does not collect, store or process personal data of any other users, followers or viewers.

Where the data is stored

Tokens are kept only on the owner’s own computer in a private file. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Data deletion

To delete all data of the app: remove the app in Facebook → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites); its tokens stop working immediately, and the owner deletes the local token file. You can also send a message to the “Its Neiron” Facebook Page asking for deletion.

Contact

Questions: send a message to the “Its Neiron” Facebook Page.
